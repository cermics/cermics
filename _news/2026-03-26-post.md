title: Vincent Boulard receives the 2026 Perronet Prize
---

 <a href="https://vincentboulard.fr" target="_blank">Vincent Boulard</a>, a PhD student at CERMICS and LJLL, receives the <a id="OWAce14348e-0b80-289a-ce6f-1e29339f520b" class="OWAAutoLink" title="https://ecoledesponts.fr/actualites-evenements/ceremonie-de-remise-de-diplomes-dingenieurs" href="https://ecoledesponts.fr/actualites-evenements/ceremonie-de-remise-de-diplomes-dingenieurs" target="_blank"> 2026 Perronet Prize</a>, which is awarded each year to the best graduating engineering student of the École nationale des ponts et chaussées.
