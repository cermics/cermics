 <a href="http://leclere.github.io" target="_blank">Vincent Leclère</a> was invited to participate in the public hearing on the future of electrical networks, organized by the Parliamentary Office for the Evaluation of Scientific and Technological Choices <a id="LPlnk649558" title="https://www2.assemblee-nationale.fr/15/les-delegations-comite-et-office-parlementaire/office-parlementaire-d-evaluation-des-choix-scientifiques-et-technologiques" href="https://www2.assemblee-nationale.fr/15/les-delegations-comite-et-office-parlementaire/office-parlementaire-d-evaluation-des-choix-scientifiques-et-technologiques" target="_blank"> (OPECST - Senate and National Assembly)</a>. He presented the various mathematical challenges (and potential solutions) posed by the ongoing ecological transition: optimal management and valuation of flexibility to address uncertainties; better consideration of network physics; and the construction of efficient, robust, and fair market mechanisms.
 
  You can view the intervention on the <a id="LPlnk547590" title="https://videos.senat.fr/video.5179701_67dbbebd7bb80.audition-publique-sur-les-reseaux-electriques?timecode=8255000" href="https://videos.senat.fr/video.5179701_67dbbebd7bb80.audition-publique-sur-les-reseaux-electriques?timecode=8255000" target="_blank"> Senate's website</a>.
