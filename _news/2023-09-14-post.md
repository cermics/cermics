---
title: Julien Guyon is interviewed by Risk Magazine
---

 <a href="https://cermics.enpc.fr/~guyon/" target="_blank">Julien Guyon</a> gave a one-hour interview to Risk magazine in August 2023. In this edition of Quantcast, Risk's quantitative podcast, Julien discusses volatility modeling and option pricing as well as World Cup draws and formats. You can listen <a href="https://www.risk.net/cutting-edge/7957389/podcast-julien-guyon-on-volatility-modelling-and-world-cup-draws" target="_blank">here</a> to the podcast.
