 On June 17th, 2025, during the <a href="https://verolog2025.unitn.it/" target="_blank">VeRoLog2025 conference</a>, the prize for the best PhD thesis in Operations Research applied to Routing and Logistics in Europe was awarded. <a href="https://louisbouvier.github.io/" target="_blank">Louis Bouvier</a>, who completed his PhD with an industrial contract with Renault Group and was supervised by <a href="https://axelparmentier.github.io/" target="_blank">Axel Parmentier</a> at CERMICS, <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/posts/fabien-lehu%C3%A9d%C3%A9-5a130217a_last-week-we-had-the-great-pleasure-of-attending-activity-7342891945997856768-U5xH?utm_source=share&amp;utm_medium=member_desktop&amp;rcm=ACoAACXz9jIBV4LsYT3fplRZG4gWpG4wfQTbOhc" target="_blank">co-received the prize</a>. Both his methodological contributions in Decision-Focused Learning and his practical applications at Renault Group were hailed.
