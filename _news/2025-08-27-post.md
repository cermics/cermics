 On Wednesday, September 10, <a href="https://team.inria.fr/matherials/team-members/virginie-ehrlacher-galland/" target="_blank">Virginie Ehrlacher</a>, researcher at CERMICS, will be awarded the <a href="https://www.legiondhonneur.fr/fr/les-decorations/les-decorations-francaises/lordre-national-du-merite" target="_blank">National Order of Merit</a> Medal. The medal will be presented by <a href="https://www.ihes.fr/professeur/laure-saint-raymond/" target="_blank">Laure Saint-Raymond</a>, Professor at IHES. On this occasion, Laure Saint-Raymond will give a talk at the CERMICS Applied Mathematics Seminar at 2:30 pm in the seminar room. The medal ceremony will follow at 4:00 pm. During the ceremony, the <a href="https://amopa.asso.fr/lordre-des-palmes-academiques/" target="_blank">Palmes Académiques</a> will also be awarded to Isabelle Simunic, General Secretary of the laboratory, and Carolina Garcia Olmeido, Head of the Contracts Office at ENPC. The afternoon will conclude with a friendly reception.
