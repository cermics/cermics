---
title: Alexandre Ern&#039;s paper (M2AN, 2016) received Frontiers of Science Award
---

 A. Ern's paper on <a href="https://www.esaim-m2an.org/articles/m2an/abs/2016/03/m2an150026/m2an150026.html" target="_blank"> Bridging the hybrid high-order and hybridizable discontinuous Galerkin methods</a> (M2AN, 2016, with B. Cockburn and D. Di Pietro) was one of the three papers in Numerical Analysis receiving the Frontiers of Science Award at the <a href="http://www.icbs.cn" target="_blank">International Congress of Basic Science</a> (Beijing, 2024).
