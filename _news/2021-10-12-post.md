 <a href="https://urbain.vaes.uk/research/" target="_blank">Urbain Vaes</a> joined the Inria project-team <a href="https://team.inria.fr/matherials/" target="_blank">MATHERIALS </a>on October 1st, 2021. His research focus is on the development and analysis of sampling methods in high dimensions, in particular for applications related to Bayesian inverse problems and molecular dynamics. He obtained his PhD in 2019 under the supervision of <a href="http://wwwf.imperial.ac.uk/~pavl/" target="_blank">Grigorios Pavliotis</a> and <a href="https://www.imperial.ac.uk/people/s.kalliadasis" target="_blank">Serafim Kalliadasis</a>. After that, he was a postdoctoral research associate in the group of <a href="http://www.imperial.ac.uk/applied-numerical-analysis" target="_blank"> Applied and Numerical Analysis</a> at <a href="http://www.imperial.ac.uk/" target="_blank">Imperial College London</a> and then at MATHERIALS for one year.
