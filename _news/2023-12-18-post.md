On December 18, 2023, <a href="https://cermics.enpc.fr/~guyon/" target="_blank">Julien Guyon</a> was live on the French TV network La chaîne L'Équipe. In what might well be a TV world premiere, he commented on <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6frczKxeeSs" target="_blank">the draw probabilities of the UEFA Champions League</a>, <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pArs8aVoRTk&amp;pp=ygUWbGVxdWlwZSB0aXJhZ2UgYXUgc29ydA%3D%3D" target="_blank">Europa League</a>, and Europa Conference League, and how they evolve during the draw. Due to the draw constraints and draw procedure, the probabilities are not easy to compute! The <a style="color: #005a95; text-decoration: none; cursor: pointer;" href="https://julienguyon.github.io/UEFA-draws/" target="_blank">live probability calculator</a> is joint work with <a href="https://cermics.enpc.fr/~meuniefr/" target="_blank">Frédéric Meunier</a> and second year École des Ponts students: <a href="https://fr.linkedin.com/in/flavien-audibert-10a581266" target="_blank">Flavien Audibert</a>, <a href="https://fr.linkedin.com/in/bilel-benaich-a6775224b" target="_blank">Bilel Benaich</a>, <a href="https://fr.linkedin.com/in/yohann-canavese-501396269" target="_blank">Yohann Canavese</a>, and <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/akram-lamssyah-582046252/" target="_blank">Akram Lamssyah</a>. The TDLOG course team (<a href="https://uk.linkedin.com/in/xavier-clerc-b0a4a495/en" target="_blank">Xavier Clerc</a> <a href="https://www.hollved.net/en/" target="_blank">Etienne Polack</a>) also contributed, as well as Edgar Duc and Antony Feord (via Ponts Etudes Projets). 
 
Julien also explained how the probabilities are computed in <a href="https://www.lemonde.fr/sport/article/2023/12/18/un-psg-bayern-munich-en-huitiemes-de-finale-de-la-ligue-des-champions-probable-mais_6206430_3242.html" target="_blank">this article in Le Monde</a>, which even includes <a href="https://julienguyon.github.io/UEFA-draws/ExactProbabilities_ChampionsLeague_RoundOf16_text_website-1.pdf" target="_blank">a nerdy link</a> to a detailed mathematical explanation---a nice mix of probability theory and graph theory.
