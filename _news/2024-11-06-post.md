 François Charton (PhD student at CERMICS and research engineer at Meta) and <a href="http://cermics.enpc.fr/~hayata/" target="_blank">Amaury Hayat</a>'s (researcher at CERMICS) <a href="https://openreview.net/pdf?id=kOMrm4ZJ3m" target="_blank">work</a> was <a href="https://www.newscientist.com/article/2452780-meta-ai-tackles-maths-problems-that-stumped-humans-for-over-a-century/" target="_blank">featured in the New Scientist</a>. In collaboration with Alberto Alfarano (research engineer at Meta), their work consist in training deep language models to find global Lyapunov function for systems of ordinary differential equations, a mathematical problem open in general.
