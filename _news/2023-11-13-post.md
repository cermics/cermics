 The 7th edition of <a href="https://kiro.enpc.org/" target="_blank">KIRO</a> was organized at École des Ponts on Thursday 9th November. KIRO is a hackathon centering on techniques and applications of operations research. This unique event among the French Grandes Écoles is organized each year by the operation research group at CERMICS (Axel Parmentier and his PhD students) together with the <a href="https://clubinfo.enpc.org/" target="_blank">Club Informatique de l'École des Ponts</a>, the <a href="https://www.fondationdesponts.fr/" target="_blank">Fondation des Ponts</a>, and a company who proposes the problem to be solved. The 7th edition was sponsored by <a href="https://www.rte-france.com/" target="_blank">RTE</a> and focused on the design of a network of offshore windmills. Students from all over France participated. Laureates came from Université de Lorraine, Télécom Paris, Centrale Paris, ENS Saclay, and École des Ponts.
