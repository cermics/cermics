 François Charton, research engineer at Meta and PhD student at CERMICS, Jordan Ellenberg (U. Wisconsin), Adam Wagner (Deepmind), Geordie Williamson (U. Sydney), were interviewed by the New Scientist on their <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00566" target="_blank">recent paper</a> where they design an AI method called PatternBoost to generate interesting construction in mathematics coupling Transformers and classical search algorithm (see the <a href="https://www.newscientist.com/article/2456653-ai-maths-assistant-could-help-solve-problems-that-humans-are-stuck-on/" target="_blank">news</a>). As an example of application, they find a counter-example to a conjecture of 1992 due to Graham.
