---
title: Frédéric Meunier president of ROADEF
---

 On February 25, 2026, Frédéric Meunier was appointed president of the <a href="https://roadef.org/" target="_blank">Roadef</a>, the French Operations Research and Decision Support Society, for a two-year term. The mission of the society is to promote the scientific field of Operations Research and Decision Support in France.
