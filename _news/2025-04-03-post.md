 Étienne Bernard was interviewed by Le Monde as part of a <a href="https://www.lemonde.fr/sciences/article/2025/04/01/signer-la-science-le-parcours-du-combattant-des-sourds-pour-acceder-a-la-recherche_6589474_1650684.html" target="_blank">report on deaf academics</a>. He shared his experience as a disabled mathematician. In particular, he talked about the difficulty of doing research as a deaf non-English speaker in an environment where spoken English is crucial to a “normal” career.
