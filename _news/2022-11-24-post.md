 Dylan Dronnier received on November 16th 2022 the <a href="https://www.paris-est-sup.fr/evenements/evenement/a1cae1856449282f59e2621fabc21372/?tx_news_pi1%5Bnews%5D=2082&amp;tx_news_pi1%5Bcontroller%5D=News&amp;tx_news_pi1%5Baction%5D=detail&amp;tx_news_pi1%5Bday%5D=16&amp;tx_news_pi1%5Bmonth%5D=11&amp;tx_news_pi1%5Byear%5D=2022" target="_blank">Prix spécial « Territoire » de la Communauté d'agglomération Paris-Vallée</a> de la Marne for his PhD thesis on <a href="https://theses.fr/2021ENPC0025" target="_blank">"Epidemics models in infinite-dimension and optimal vaccination strategies"</a>. Dylan Dronnier is a former student of École des Ponts, and completed his PhD under the co-supervision of Jean-François Delmas (CERMICS) and Pierre-André Zitt (LAMA).
