 <a href="https://www.fondationdesponts.fr/">Fondation des Ponts</a> awards the <a href="https://www.fondationdesponts.fr/prix-pasquet-guillaume-dalle/">Pasquet</a> prize to the "best" student who has graduated from an engineering program. Guillaume DALLE (IPEF engineer) is preparing his thesis in the Optimization team on prediction and planning problems in the railway system, with approaches that combine machine learning and operations research. He was congratulated for his thoroughness, his maturity, his excellence and his investment in teaching and in collective actions.
