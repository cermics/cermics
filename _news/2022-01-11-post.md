---
title: Louis Bouvier was co-awarded the 2021 ROADEF Master&#039;s Thesis
---

 The <a href="https://www.roadef.org/">ROADEF</a> RO/AD Master's Thesis Prize aims to highlight the research of Master's students devoted to Operational Research and Decision Analysis.

Louis Bouvier, a student in the Master 2 <a href="https://www.master-mva.com/">MVA</a> program at <a href="https://ens-paris-saclay.fr/">ENS Paris-Saclay</a> and <a href="https://www.ecoledesponts.fr">École des Ponts ParisTech</a>, was awarded a prize for his dissertation entitled 'Large Neighborhood Search and Structured Prediction for the Inventory Routing Problem'.

The jury was impressed by the quality of the work presented and particularly appreciated the scientific content of the work, the ability to present it with pedagogy and the degree of hindsight that appeared in the answers to questions.
