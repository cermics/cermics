 <a href="https://www.fondationdesponts.fr/">Fondation des Ponts</a> awards the Pasquet prize to the "best" student who has graduated from an engineering program. It was awarded in 2022 to Régis Santet. See <a href="https://twitter.com/Fondation_Ponts/status/1535269426576113678" target="_blank">here</a> for further elements. <a href="https://rsantet.github.io/" target="_blank">Régis Santet</a> is currently preparing his thesis in the Modeling, Analysis and Simulation team, on the development of efficient numerical methods for molecular dynamics.
