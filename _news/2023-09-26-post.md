 Meta AI just released Code Llama, a large language model dedicated to coding. This software is based on Llama 2, and is one of the most performant open source options currently available. It is in particular a direct competitor to ChatGPT and Bing AI to generate new codes, and/or explain or comment lines of code. 
 
 
 Fabian Glöckle, PhD student at CERMICS, is one of the five main co-authors of the associated <a href="https://ai.meta.com/research/publications/code-llama-open-foundation-models-for-code/" target="_blank">research paper</a>. See the <a href="https://ai.meta.com/blog/code-llama-large-language-model-coding/" target="_blank">blog post</a> of Meta for further details.
