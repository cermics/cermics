---
title: Arrival of Laura Grazioli (CNRS researcher)
---

<a href="https://lauragrazioli.github.io/">Laura GRAZIOLI</a> joined our lab as a CNRS researcher from 1st October. She was trained as a theoretical chemist, obtaining her PhD at Johannes Gutenberg-Universität, Mainz, under the supervision of Prof. Ju4rgen Gauss and Prof. Stella Stopkowicz. She later decided to deepen her knowledge of the mathematical aspects of quantum chemistry, joining us as a postdoc from 2024. Her research interests are mainly in the domain of wave function theory for the description of electronic states, placing her research at the interface between theoretical chemistry and applied mathematics.
