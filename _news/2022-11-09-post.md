In November 2022, Julien Guyon published <a
href="https://www.lemonde.fr/sport/article/2022/11/07/ligue-des-champions-le-bayern-munich-adversaire-le-plus-probable-du-psg-en-huitiemes-de-finale_6148779_3242.html"
target="_blank">an article</a> in Le Monde and was <a
href="https://www.lequipe.fr/Football/Article/Le-bayern-munich-adversaire-le-plus-probable-pour-le-psg-en-8e-de-ligue-des-champions/1362704"
target="_blank">interviewed</a> in L'Equipe. He explained how to compute the
draw probabilities of the round of 16 of the UEFA Champions League, a neat math
problem which passionates football fans and is not as easy to solve as it looks!
His calculations were also presented in the popular French TV program <a
href="https://live.lequipe.fr/replay/l-equipe-du-soir-du-2-novembre-2eme-partie/20163672"
target="_blank">L'Equipe du Soir</a>.
		
Julien Guyon has extensively published articles on fairness in sports in top-tier journals, including The New York Times, Le Monde, The Times, El País, as well as in academic journals; a list is available on his <a href="https://cermics.enpc.fr/~guyon/" target="_blank">web page</a>. Topics have included:
		
 - <a href="http://ssrn.com/abstract=2424376" target="_blank">proposition of fairer draw procedures for the FIFA World Cup</a>, one of which was adopted by FIFA in 2018. The whole story was summarized in <a href="https://theconversation.com/pourquoi-la-coupe-du-monde-est-plus-equitable-cette-annee-97948" target="_blank">The Conversation</a>. <a href="https://content.iospress.com/articles/journal-of-sports-analytics/jsa219" target="_blank">a fairer knockout bracket for the UEFA Euro</a>, also adopted by UEFA in 2020 
 
 - <a href="https://content.iospress.com/articles/journal-of-sports-analytics/jsa200414" target="_blank">risk of collusion for the 2026 FIFA World Cup</a> (48 teams, 16 groups of 3) and proposition of fairer formats
