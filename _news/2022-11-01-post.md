 <a href="https://cermics.enpc.fr/~lebris/" target="_blank">Claude Le Bris</a> will be a <a href="https://mathplus.de/research-2/visiting-scholars/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">MATH+ Distinguished Visiting Scholar</a> for the academic year 2022/2023. He will be hosted by <a href="https://www.mi.fu-berlin.de/en/math/groups/ag-numerik/members/Kornhuber/kornhuber.html">Ralf Kornhuber</a> of Freie Universität Berlin. The title “Distinguished Visiting Scholar” is given by the Berlin Mathematics Research Center to outstanding scientists and scholars from around the world with high international visibility.
