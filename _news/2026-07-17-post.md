Julien Guyon wrote in the French newspaper
[L'Equipe](https://www.lequipe.fr/Football/Actualites/La-france-a-t-elle-ete-vraiment-malchanceuse-lors-du-tirage-au-sort-de-la-coupe-du-monde-ce-que-disent-les-maths/1683895)
a brief, accessible overview of the fundamental concepts of bias, variance, and
randomness, using the 2026 World Cup football draw as an example: Which team was
the luckiest/unluckiest during the draw? And also in English in the quantitative
finance magazine
[Risk](https://www.risk.net/cutting-edge/views/7963631/vinicius-fortune-quantifying-luck-in-the-world-cup-draw). This
is joint work with Thomas Buchholtzer, a student in the IMI department at École
des Ponts, who is set to begin a PhD this fall.
