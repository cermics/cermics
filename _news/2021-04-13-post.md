 <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/gferre/accueil" target="_blank">Grégoire Ferré</a> was a PhD student at CERMICS from October 2016 to September 2019, and defended his PhD in November 2019. He worked on large deviation theory and its application in statistical physics, both from theoretical and numerical viewpoints. He now is a research associate at <a class="XqQF9c" href="https://www.cfm.fr/who-we-are/" target="_blank">Capital Fund Management.</a>
