 <a href="https://gaspardkemlin.frama.io/">Gaspard Kemlin</a> was a PhD student at CERMICS from September 2019 to November 2022, and defended his PhD in December 2022. He worked on Numerical analysis for Kohn-Sham Density Functional Theory. He now is an associate professor at <a href="https://www.u-picardie.fr/"> Picardie Jules Verne University.</a>
