Andrew McRae, together with Nicolas Boumal (EPFL, Switzerland), received the [2026 SIAM Activity Group on Optimization Best Paper Prize](https://www.siam.org/programs-initiatives/prizes-awards/activity-group-prizes/siam-activity-group-on-optimization-early-career-prize/). This prize is awarded every three years to the author(s) of the most outstanding paper, as determined by the prize committee, on a topic in optimization published in the four calendar years preceding the award year.

Andrew and Nicolas received the prize for their paper "[Benign Landscapes of Low-Dimensional Relaxations for Orthogonal Synchronization on General Graphs](https://doi.org/10.1137/23M1584642)". This work provides rigorous theoretical guarantees on the nonconvex landscape of a low-dimensional relaxation for orthogonal group synchronization problems, which appear in many practical applications such as robotics, imaging, and computer vision.

See the [interview](https://www.siam.org/publications/siam-news/articles/2026-may-prize-spotlight) with the prize winners.
