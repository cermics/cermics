The operations research and machine learning chair is a long term academic-industrial partnership between Air France (the main French airline) and École des Ponts ParisTech (one of the leading French Grandes Écoles). Theoretical contributions and applications to industrial problems are in the scope of the chair. Research focuses on the following themes:

 -   Operations Research and Combinatorial Optimization.
 -   Machine Learning.
 -   Stochastic Combinatorial Optimization.

The coordinators of the chair are Pierre-Cyril Aubin and Axel Parmentier.
