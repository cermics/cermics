### Optimization Team

The Optimization team works on models, methods, and applications of optimization. Areas of expertise are discrete optimization and stochastic optimization. Applications cover various fields: biodiversity, energy, risk, supply chain, sustainability, transportation.

The team has strong experience in industrial collaborations and has established long-lasting relations with several leading companies in energy and transportation.
