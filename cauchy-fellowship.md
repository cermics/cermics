<p> CERMICS, the research center in
applied mathematics of École nationale des ponts et chaussées, Paris, is pleased
to announce a two-year post-doctoral position in mathematics. This position
comes with a net salary from 27000€ per year (which can be adjusted depending on
the experience of the selected candidate) and is free of teaching
duties. However, candidates interested in consolidating their teaching
experience will have the opportunity to give lectures at École nationale des
ponts et chaussées. Moreover, the selected candidate will get a working budget
of 5000€ per year. The position is available for commencement anytime between
September 1st and December 15th, 2025.

Candidates are strongly encouraged to suggest collaborations with permanent
researchers at CERMICS in their research activities. Additionally, a personal
scientific agenda related to the areas covered by CERMICS —mathematical analysis
of deterministic and stochastic models, numerical analysis, optimization,
operations research, probability, discrete mathematics, etc.— is very much
welcome.

For more details regarding living conditions in France, taxes, health insurance,
teaching opportunities, working budget, starting dates, visa applications, etc.,
do not hesitate to <a href="mailto:cauchy@liste.enpc.fr">contact us</a>.  
</p>

### Important dates
The deadline for applications is January 31st, 2025. The result will be announced in March 2025.

### Who can apply?
Individuals with a PhD or those preparing a PhD in mathematics, computer
science, or related topics, of any nationality, are eligible to apply. However,
the selected candidate must have defended the PhD thesis before the beginning of
the post-doctoral contract.

### How to apply?
Interested candidates must contact a <strong>permanent researcher</strong> at
CERMICS. Upon endorsement of the application by the researcher, candidates are
expected to prepare a research project in collaboration with him or her. The
list of researchers at CERMICS is available <a href="{{ '/staff/' | relative_url
}}">here</a>.

<br>
{% include staff_gallery.html %}

The candidate must then submit a single pdf file at <a
href="mailto:cauchy@liste.enpc.fr">cauchy@liste.enpc.fr</a> containing the
following elements:

- A cover page summarizing the application:
    - Name of the contacted permanent researcher at CERMICS.
    - Desired starting date.
    - Title of the research project.
- A resume with the publication list.
- The research project (maximum two pages).
- At least two recommendation letters, or the name of at least two references, who will send the recommendation letters directly to the above email address.
